# Week 12 Homework: Final Demonstration and Job-Ready Portfolio

## Goal and Starting Point
Finalize and present the production project. Consolidate earlier evidence into a navigable repository; do not manufacture a new large feature at the deadline.

Bring the production repository, deployment/release record, incident exercise, workflow SOP, and interview feedback. Freeze feature scope before rehearsing; prioritize broken core behavior and evidence gaps.

## Required Acceptance Evidence
- A reproducible repository with setup/run/check commands, architecture diagram, API contract, and migration instructions.
- A working demonstration and clear deployment status; any unavailable hosted service is labeled honestly.
- A success and failure-path demonstration supported by relevant automated checks.
- One before/after engineering case study showing the actual change and evidence.
- An explanation of AI responsibilities, your review/intervention decisions, and reused material.
- Known limitations, operating/cleanup instructions, a concise project narrative, and a next-step practice plan.

## Work Budget: 5–10 Hours
- Planning and baseline review: 1–2h.
- Main implementation or exercise: 2–4h.
- Independent checks and repair: 1–2h.
- Demonstration, reflection, and submission: 1–2h.

Report each required criterion as met, partial, or unmet with evidence. Prioritize broken core behavior before optional extensions.

## Submission
Submit the final repository/PR link, demo or recording, release/runbook, case study, and `docs/prompt-logs/week-12.md`. Include links to the SOP and incident report so reviewers can inspect the progression.

Use the [shared evidence-log template](../../../infrastructure/template/docs/prompt-logs/TEMPLATE.md). Share the PR/repository link to your own repository by the final presentation. Disclose reused material and explain your contribution.

## Assessment
The [shared engineering rubric](../../../tools/agentic-mentor/evaluation-rubric.md) applies: context management, validation and correction, structural oversight, and system integrity, each worth 25%. Assess these against the required evidence above; missing evidence keeps the affected score provisional.

## Next
Students leave with a portfolio package and a practice plan; mentors identify remaining gaps rather than treating a graduation demo as proof of every skill.
