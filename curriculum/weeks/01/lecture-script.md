# Week 1 Presenter Notes

## Opening (10m)
“This residency assumes you can already code and use Git. The next step is learning to direct and verify AI-assisted engineering. Today, three people without traditional technical backgrounds will show their finished work and how their methods changed. Notice the process as much as the product.”

Explain GitHub-only distribution, bring-your-own paid agent access in VS Code and terminal, and 5–10 hours of weekly independent work.

## Speakers (75m)
Introduce each speaker and their domain. Listen for a first attempt, a turning point, and a present-day method. Note two contrasting approaches for the panel.

## Q&A (25m)
Ask: What stopped working as the project grew? How did you know the AI was wrong? What do you now do before asking it to build? What would you change if starting today? Invite student questions.

## Closing (10m)
“Your homework is to get your tools working and try something at home. Bring evidence and questions, even if it fails. Next week we start a fresh project in VS Code and introduce a more deliberate method.” Point students to `homework.md`.
