# Week 1 Speaker and Session Guide

Invite **three nontechnical builders**. Each has 25 minutes for presentation and finished-work demonstration, followed by a shared 25-minute Q&A. No classroom project build is planned.

## Speaker Brief
Please show what you built and the evolution of how you built it:
1. The finished product and who it serves.
2. Your first workflow: tools, prompts, and what you did manually.
3. A concrete failure, including what you changed afterward.
4. Your current workflow, with screenshots or a session excerpt.
5. How you check results and what you still struggle with.

A prepared walkthrough is sufficient; a live change is optional, not required. Keep a recording or screenshots available if the product cannot be demonstrated live. Scrub credentials and private customer data.

## Instructor Preparation
Collect guest slides/demo links for the GitHub materials with their permission. Check presentation access and timing. Prepare questions about iteration and verification, not only impressive outputs. Finish by explaining the at-home setup and field report; the first classroom build happens in Week 2.
