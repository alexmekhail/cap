# Week 1: Inspiration — The Art of the Possible

## Learning Goals
See what nontechnical people have built with AI, understand how their methods evolved, and form questions for your own first attempt. There is no project build in class.

## Session Agenda (120 minutes)
- **Welcome and framing (10m):** Explain the job-readiness goal, 5–10 hour weekly workload, GitHub distribution, and day-one tool access.
- **Three nontechnical speakers (75m):** Each has 25 minutes for a presentation and finished-product walkthrough, emphasizing workflow evolution and mistakes.
- **Joint Q&A (25m):** Ask about their first approach, what stopped working, how they validated results, and what changed their method.
- **Debrief and homework briefing (10m):** Connect common patterns and explain the at-home setup and exploratory attempt. No classroom project or full tool tutorial.
